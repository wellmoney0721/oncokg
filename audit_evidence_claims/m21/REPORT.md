**Identity premise and evidence:** a Ce-134-labelled trastuzumab conjugate is a real, distinct research material. Bailey et al., *Evaluation of 134Ce as a PET imaging surrogate for antibody drug conjugates incorporating 225Ac* (2022; online 2022-04-22; DOI 10.1016/j.nucmedbio.2022.04.007), studied `[134Ce]Ce-DOTA-Trastuzumab` in tumour-bearing mice. The short plain-English query's relationship to this tracer family is the reviewer's inference. The paper establishes product distinction; it does not supply graph edges or clinical claims. [Primary paper abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35512517/).

**Cause:** this is an intentional recall tradeoff documented at `src/query.py:133-136` and in the repair notes. `src/query.py:124-136` puts Ce in `_NAME_MASS` but excludes it from `_MEDICAL_MASS`; `src/textrules.py:290-299` also excludes `ce`. The written-name branch at `src/query.py:208-220` recognizes `cerium`, while the explicit `Ce-134` and `[134Ce]` tokens remain invisible. The item guard at `src/query.py:457-459` does not fire, `_marked` ultimately returns `None` at `src/query.py:485`, the linker adds a Drug at `src/query.py:613-630`, and `Engine.explore` selects it at `src/app.py:88-95`.

Avoiding false positives for `75 several` and `51 criteria` does not justify accepting a whole bracketed isotope or explicit `labelled with Ce-134`. A repair must distinguish bounded isotope tokens from prefixes of ordinary words. Required outcome: refusal or the correct distinct product, with no unlabelled-parent map/QA substitution. No implementation was changed in this review.

**Same-cause corroboration:** `trastuzumab labelled with Mn-52` likewise returns the parent map and both evidence arrays, whereas `trastuzumab labelled with manganese-52` refuses. A 52Mn-labelled trastuzumab construct is documented in Omweri et al. (2024), DOI 10.1007/s11307-024-01948-4. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11436409/). The immediate `52Mn trastuzumab` form refuses and is not claimed as a leak. Er/Se/Cr/Co/La/Fe/Cs observations are retained as software probes; their isotope/trastuzumab pairings are not needed to prove this blocker.
