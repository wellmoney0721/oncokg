## M25-B1 — three-word negation suppresses a separately affirmative conjugation (P1)

**Exact input: `trastuzumab not radiolabelled but conjugated to DM1`.** The sentence negates radiolabelling and affirmatively describes DM1 conjugation. The current linker emits bare TRASTUZUMAB; the previous query module refuses it. The HTTP map and both QA evidence arrays are identical to bare trastuzumab, and the form draws CHEMBL1201585 in the SVG and trail. `non-radioactive trastuzumab conjugated to DM1` independently reproduces the same mechanism. [Direct current/previous evidence](attacks/new_results.json), [HTTP assertions](attacks/http_assertions.json), [selected callbacks](contract/selected_current_callbacks.json).

**Causal hypothesis:** the new negation scope counts words instead of the negated joining phrase. `_item_joined` at `src/query.py:246-251` adds the next three token positions to `negated` after any `_NOT_WORDS` token. After excluding the matched drug name, the primary remainder is `not radiolabelled but conjugated to DM1`; the three suppressed words are **radiolabelled / but / conjugated**. The affirmative joining stem is skipped, `_item_joined=False`, and `_marked` at `src/query.py:569-571` returns None. `link_entities` returns the unblocked hit at `src/query.py:423-432`; `Engine.explore` selects it at `src/app.py:88-95`. The isotope guard correctly negates radiolabelling, so it cannot rescue the missed conjugation.

**Causal check:** an in-memory experiment shortening only this joining-negation span makes these positive-conjugation examples refuse. It does not cure the separate linked-to mechanism. That simplification also loses desired multiword negation behavior, which is why it is a diagnostic ablation rather than a proposed fix. [Ablation](attacks/counterfactual.json), [script](attacks/counterfactual.py).

**Identity premise:** NCI describes ado-trastuzumab emtansine as an antibody-drug conjugate containing trastuzumab joined to DM1 through an MCC linker. This supports the constituent-versus-conjugate distinction. The audit text does not specify the exact linker and therefore need not resolve uniquely to emtansine; it must not silently become bare trastuzumab. [NCI Drug Dictionary](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-drug/def/trastuzumab-emtansine).

Paired controls `trastuzumab conjugated to DM1` and `trastuzumab linked to DM1` refuse, while the genuinely negated `trastuzumab not covalently linked to DM1` retains the parent. Required outcome: preserve the genuine multiword negation control without letting a later affirmative conjugation cross the negation boundary.

**Cause:** `_item_joined` at `src/query.py:254-263` recognizes `linked` with `via`/`linker` in a short lookahead, but for `linked to/with` checks **only `raw[i+2]`** for a digit, all-uppercase spelling, or a lowercase-to-uppercase transition. It tests **a**, never the following **DM1**. A naturally lowercase reagent also fails this heuristic: `trastuzumab linked to polyethylene glycol` is newly accepted, and **`irinotecan linked to polyethylene glycol`** remains accepted from M24. The latter returns **CHEMBL481**, five graph trials and identical **five trial / four sponsor QA rows** to bare irinotecan. Both have empty blocked sets and no Refused entity.

Restoring only unconditional `linked to/with` recognition in memory closes these examples but over-refuses the intended relational control `osimertinib linked to resistance`. The negation ablation does not close them. These paired experiments distinguish two causes and establish why indiscriminate refusal is not a complete repair. [Ablations](attacks/counterfactual.json).

NCI's etirinotecan-pegol definition explicitly distinguishes irinotecan attached through a linker to polyethylene glycol from released irinotecan. This independently supports the historical residual's identity premise; it does not require the generic audit text to identify a unique PEG conjugate. [NCI Drug Dictionary](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-drug/def/etirinotecan-pegol). The DM1 identity premise is the same NCI definition cited for M25-B1.

Required outcome: recognize the explicit payload/reagent noun phrase without relying only on its first token's capitalization, while retaining ordinary relation wording. The article, lowercase reagent and historical PEG variants are grouped as one mechanism, not counted as separate blockers.
