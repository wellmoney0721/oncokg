## STATISTICAL OBJECTIONS

Independent calculation confirms the original **105/115 = 91.3043%**, Wilson **84.7303–95.2082%**.

Unique-resolution weighting matches the sampler (`make_gold_sample.py:79–97`); it does not estimate precision over trial occurrences or graph edges. However, `evaluate.py:65–71` combines **current population weights with historical labels**: 20/172 test rows changed, including 12 accepted rows and every originally incorrect accepted match.

Carried accepting tiers contribute **22/2,185 = 1.0069%**. FUZZY_REVIEW contributes zero. Excluding carried tiers gives **97.0766%**, versus 96.8772%.

A weighted sum of marginal Wilson endpoints (`evaluate.py:116–120`) is not a calibrated 95% interval. A 200,000-resample stratified bootstrap gives **94.23–98.95%**, versus published **85.66–98.97%**. Bootstrap cannot discover errors in empirically perfect tiers, so it is no replacement. Postfix **71.49–99.78%** reproduces but does not establish repair success.
